Portals in Italy, Spain and France leave out the address, or blur the pin across half a kilometre. The listing still gives the house away: the plot size, the floor area, the energy certificate. Locate reads those details and matches them against the public cadastre and energy-certificate registers. You get the parcel on an aerial photo, ranked, with how sure it is and why.

How it works

Three steps, from listing to parcel.

  1. Paste the listing.

    A link, or the listing text. Some portals refuse automated requests; when that happens it says so and asks for the text instead.

  2. Check what it read.

    Town, street, plot and garden size, floor area, energy class and consumption, year, floor, and any map pins from the portals. You correct a field before it searches, not after.

  3. Get ranked parcels.

    Each candidate comes with its cadastral reference, area, distance from the listing's location, a confidence level and the reasons behind it, on an aerial photo with the cadastre outlines. A candidate is marked high only when it is clearly ahead of the next one. When two independent sources name the same parcel, that counts in its favour.

Coverage

Where it can find flats, and where only houses.

Houses are found by plot size on the cadastre, wherever the cadastre is open. Flats need more: a public register of energy certificates with an address, so the listing's consumption figure points to one building. Only some regions publish one.

Italy

  • LombardyHouses by plot size, flats by energy certificate.
  • PiedmontHouses by plot size, flats by energy certificate. Its 774,736 certificates name the parcel directly.
  • Liguria, Tuscany, Emilia-Romagna, Veneto, Valle d'AostaNo certificate data with an address. Houses by plot size only.
  • Anywhere with a portal pinThe parcel under the pin, checked against the plot size.

Spain

  • CataloniaEnergy certificates with the cadastral reference, checked against the Catastro.
  • Rest of Spain, except Navarra and the Basque CountryCatastro address and plot-size search. No certificate source yet.

France

  • Certificates since July 202115.9 million energy certificates with address, type and floor area, then the parcel from the national cadastre.
  • Older certificatesNot in this register yet, so those listings come back without a strong match.

Who it's for

For people who look at other people's listings for a living.

A client sends a link. The address is hidden, the pin is a circle, and the agent behind it is slow to answer. You want to know which house it is before you spend a day on it.

  • Buyer's agents. Know the house, the plot and its neighbours before you book the viewing.
  • Property finders. Check a shortlist of listings against the map before you drive to them.
  • Relocation consultants. Tell a client where a listing actually sits: which street, which side of town.
Honest limits

Estates spread over several parcels aren't found yet. In Piedmont, one of four live listings tested had its certificate in the public data. Below “high”, check the result against the listing's photos. It finds the parcel or building; it does not find the owner.
