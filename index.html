How it works

Three steps, one answer.

  1. Send the listing.

    A link, or the text of the ad.

  2. We match it to the public records.

    An ad gives the house away: the garden size, the floor area, the energy figure. We check those details against Italy's public land registry and energy certificates.

  3. You get the location within a day.

    The address, a pin on the map, and a confidence level: high, medium or low. If we can't find it, we say so.

The alternative

One link, not a trip to Italy.

Without us Days, or a flight

Calls and emails to the agency, if they answer. Agencies often keep the address back until the viewing, so you book a trip to find out where the house is.

With us Within a day

One link sent. You know where the house is before you call anyone or book anything. Free during the test.

One real example

A villa on Lake Como.

The ad said
A villa in Gravedona ed Uniti, on Via Molo Vecchio, with no house number and a garden of about 1,840 m².
We found
The house on the map: a land-registry plot of 1,835 m² on that street. No other plot within 400 m comes within 10% of that size.
Confidence
High. A buyer's agent who had the answer confirmed it was the right house.
Honest limits

Some listings can't be found, and a result below “high” should be checked against the ad's photos. Flats work only in Lombardy and Piedmont, where energy certificates are public, and we find the building, not the flat. We don't look up the owner.
