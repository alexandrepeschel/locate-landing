What a check answers

Four answers, from one real check.

A villa near Todi, in Umbria, listed at €950,000.

  1. Where it is.

    The ad named only the locality, and its map pin was about 100 m off. We found the house: a land-registry plot of 4,268 m², against the ad's 4,400.

  2. The plot.

    The garden is bigger than the plot. The drive and lawns run onto three neighbouring plots, so ask who owns them.

  3. The hazards.

    A mapped high-hazard landslide zone starts 21 m from the villa. No flood zone within 700 m.

  4. The real cost.

    €2,568 per m² asked, where the tax agency values villas in that area at €800 to €1,200. And about €125,000 in taxes and fees on top of the price.
